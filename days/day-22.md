### Session overview

Let's learn how to move beyond single-response agents by implementing reflection and planning cycles that allow agents to evaluate, refine, and improve their own outputs. 

In this session, we’ll explore how iterative reasoning can make agentic solutions more reliable for complex scenarios such as financial analysis, diagnostic support, and code review.
