### Session overview

Most enterprises are racing to deploy AI agents — but few are asking what happens after launch. When 20 agents across 5 business units are calling APIs, reading sensitive data, and taking autonomous actions, governance becomes the silent crisis nobody planned for. This session is not about building your first agent. It's about what comes next: credential sprawl, rogue tool calls, no audit trail, and zero cross-team visibility.

Using Microsoft Foundry's brand-new Toolboxes (April 2026), Agent Framework 1.0, and Entra Agent Identity, we'll build a production-grade agent governance layer live on stage — covering tool discovery, least-privilege enforcement, A2A protocol controls, and OpenTelemetry tracing. Real-world patterns from regulated industry deployments (pharma/life sciences) will ground every architecture decision with authentic failure-mode stories you won't hear in vendor demos.
