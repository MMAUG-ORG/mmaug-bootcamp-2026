### Session overview

Microsoft 365 Copilot goes beyond prompting a general-purpose assistant. Specialized agents bring distinct capabilities to different types of work.

In this session, we’ll take a hands-on tour of Researcher, Analyst, and Facilitator agents, exploring how they approach research, data analysis, and collaboration. Through live demos, we’ll compare their capabilities, examine practical use cases, and discuss how to identify the right agent for the right job. Whether you’re already using Copilot or just beginning to explore agents, this session will give you practical ideas you can take back to your organization.
