### Session overview

This beginner-friendly session introduces the principles and practices required to design, develop, and use AI systems responsibly. Participants will explore fairness, reliability and safety, privacy and security, inclusiveness, transparency, accountability, and human oversight. Through practical examples, they will learn how to identify potential AI risks, evaluate model behavior, protect sensitive data, reduce bias, and introduce appropriate safeguards throughout the AI lifecycle. By the end of the session, participants will understand how to build AI solutions that are trustworthy, ethical, secure, and aligned with responsible technology practices.
