### Session overview

This session provides a beginner-friendly introduction to Artificial Intelligence, exploring what AI is, how it has evolved, and the major concepts that shape the field. Participants will learn about machine learning, deep learning, generative AI, neural networks, model training, inference, and multimodal systems. The session will also introduce commonly used AI tools, frameworks, model hubs, Microsoft Foundry, and relevant Azure AI services while examining real-world applications, limitations, and responsible AI considerations.
