### Session overview

What if your first coding partner could explain code, help you fix errors, and guide you through building your first project?

In this beginner-friendly session, we'll explore how GitHub Copilot can help you learn programming, understand unfamiliar code, and turn ideas into working software. Through practical demonstrations, we'll go from a simple prompt to building and improving a small application with AI assistance.

We'll also look beyond the “magic”: how to write effective prompts, review AI-generated code, spot mistakes and security risks, and keep a human in control. No advanced programming experience is required, just curiosity and a willingness to experiment.
