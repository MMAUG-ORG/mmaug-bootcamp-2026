### Session overview

The Prompt Craft Foundations session outlines the three key abilities needed to work effectively with AI language models: creating clear and well-structured prompts, providing the appropriate context (such as documents, memory, and tools) so that the model can give accurate answers, and systematically assessing the AI's output rather than relying on first impressions. 

The session is aimed at beginners, including students, people changing careers, and early-career technologists.
