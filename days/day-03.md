### Session overview

In these session, I will discussing the waterfall model, Agile model and how they intersect. Different company use cases and practical examples.

I will also talk about what DevOps is, the different DevOps phases, tools and DevSops advantage.

This session is to enlighten the audience on what DevOps is, it's use case and importance. How it brings everyone together in software development cycle.
